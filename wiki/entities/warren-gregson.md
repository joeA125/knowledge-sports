# Warren Gregson

First and corresponding author of [[match-to-match-variability-high-speed|Match-to-Match Variability of High-Speed Activities in Premier League Soccer]] (2010). **Research Institute for Sport and Exercise Sciences, Liverpool John Moores University.**

## Why This Page Exists

He is the vault's entry into **sports physiology and performance analysis** — the sports-science discipline, distinct from both the machine-learning football literature and the psychometrics of [[alexander-franks|Franks]].

That discipline matters to the vault for one specific reason recorded on [[match-to-match-variability]]: it is the literature that measured the **noise floor** every value metric sits on, using the [[coefficient-of-variation|CV]] register that the ML literature does not use. Gregson is a lead into it, and a productive one — the paper's co-author list and reference list point at a dense Liverpool JMU / Prozone cluster.

⚠️ What is evidenced is only the byline and affiliation. No later work or research programme is asserted; the conventions' worked example on entity affiliations applies, and this rests on a single 2010 source.

## The Co-Authors Are the Map

The paper is one node in a tightly connected group, all cited within it:

## The Companion Paper

⚠️ The paper leans repeatedly on **Di Salvo, Gregson, Atkinson, Tordoff & Drust (2009)**, *Analysis of high intensity activity in Premier League soccer* (same journal), for the underlying activity profiles this one computes variability over.

**That is the natural next acquisition** if the physical-performance line is pursued: it holds the *levels* to this paper's *variability*, on the same system and roughly the same population. The pair together would give the vault both halves — how much players run, and how much that bounces.
