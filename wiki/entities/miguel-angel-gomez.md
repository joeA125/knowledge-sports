## Methodological Signature, So Far as One Source Can Show

The paper's design has a shape the vault's other sources lack: **validate the definitions first, then measure agreement on applying them.** Aiken's V over 20 licensed coaches establishes what should be counted; Kappa, ICC and typical error establish whether it was counted consistently.

That two-step is the reason [[construct-validity]] now distinguishes expert assent to a *definition* from expert assent to a *quantity* — the first being the right use of a panel, the second a fallback. **Gong et al. is the vault's only instance of the first.**
