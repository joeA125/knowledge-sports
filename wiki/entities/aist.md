# AIST (National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology)

Japanese national research institute, based in Tsukuba. Affiliation of [[masaki-onishi]] and — **at the time of publication** — of [[atom-scott]], on [[ai-football-reinforcement-learning|Scott, Fujii & Onishi (2022)]].

## Why It Is Worth a Page

**It is the second Japanese national-institute affiliation in the vault**, alongside RIKEN AIP, and the two appear on different halves of the same research programme:

| Institute | Appears on | Register |
|---|---|---|
| **AIST** | [[ai-football-reinforcement-learning\|Scott et al. (2022)]] | [[agent-based-simulation\|Simulation]] feasibility |
| RIKEN AIP | [[adaptive-action-supervision-multi-agent-rl\|Fujii et al. (2023)]], and Fujii's standing affiliation | Multi-agent methodology |

Neither is a university, and [[keisuke-fujii]] is the bridge to both. The pattern noted on [[nagoya-university]] — that this research programme runs in an applied register and a methodological one — extends further than the university-versus-institute split suggests: **the non-university affiliations attach to the papers that treat football as a test case rather than as the subject.**

Scott et al. is the clearest instance. Its stated contribution is verifying simulation as an approach, and its football data (five J-League matches) is a comparison set rather than an object of study.

## Scott's Affiliation Moved

> **Correction recorded 2026-08-08.**

[[atom-scott]] is listed here at AIST in 2022 and at [[nagoya-university|Nagoya]] on the 2023 [[adaptive-action-supervision-multi-agent-rl|adaptive action supervision]] paper. The vault's `atom-scott` page previously asserted Nagoya without qualification, inferred from the 2023 collective affiliation list.

The chronology suggests a move from AIST to Nagoya between 2022 and 2023, but **no held source states this**, and the 2023 paper lists institutions collectively rather than per-author, so the Nagoya attribution for Scott was always weaker than it read. Recorded as ambiguous rather than resolved.
