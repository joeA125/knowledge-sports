## Acquisition Note

**Renkin et al. (2022)** on motion-model validation is the most valuable of the three unheld Vienna papers, for a specific reason: Bischofberger **declined to use motion models** in the 2026 paper, arguing that physically reachable areas overestimate involvement for players constrained by team tactics.

That is a direct argument against how the [[pitch-control]] tradition constructs reachable space, made by someone who had previously validated such models. The vault holds [[pitch-control-traditions-compared|two pitch-control traditions]] and no critique of either from outside.
