## The Prozone Line

Within the held paper he is cited as lead author on the works that make its data usable at all:

- **Di Salvo, Collins, McNeill & Cardinale (2006)**, *Validation of Prozone: a new video-based performance analysis system* — the **validation study for the tracking system** every figure in the held paper depends on
- **Di Salvo et al. (2007)**, positional performance characteristics in elite soccer
- **Di Salvo, Gregson, Atkinson, Tordoff & Drust (2009)**, high-intensity activity in the Premier League — the companion paper holding the activity *levels*
